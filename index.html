What We Do

Home & Business IT Services

A full range of consulting and support, delivered with the same care and polish as a national provider — at local, personal scale.

Computer Repair

Hardware and software diagnostics for slow, freezing, or won't-boot desktops and laptops. Screen, keyboard, and power issues fixed — most repairs turned around fast.

Network & Wi-Fi Setup

Router and mesh configuration, dead-zone elimination, and secure passwords for home or office. Wired runs, guest networks, and printer/smart-device connectivity.

Printer & Device Support

Setup and troubleshooting for printers, scanners, webcams, and peripherals. Driver fixes, wireless printing, and multi-device sharing across your network.

PC Upgrades

SSD, RAM, and component upgrades that make an older machine feel new. Windows migration and clean installs so you keep your files and speed things up.

Virus & Malware Removal

Removal of viruses, spyware, ransomware, and pop-ups — plus antivirus setup and safe-browsing tips to keep it from coming back.

Data Backup & Recovery

Automated local and cloud backups so nothing is ever lost. Recovery of deleted, corrupted, or drive-failure files whenever possible.

Remote & On-Site Support

Quick remote sessions for fast fixes, or in-person visits to your home or business across Fort Myers and Southwest Florida.
